Process

From first visit to finished yard.

What happens once you call us, step by step.

  1. 01

    Free estimate

    We visit the property, listen to what you want and look at sun, soil, drainage and irrigation.

  2. 02

    Design & proposal

    A clear plan for plants, palms and lighting, with a written, itemized price. No surprises.

  3. 03

    Installation

    One crew lead on site from the first shovel to the final walkthrough, and one number to call the whole time.

  4. 04

    Ongoing care

    Optional maintenance keeps everything healthy and sharp, season after season.
